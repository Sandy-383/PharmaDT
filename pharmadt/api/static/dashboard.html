Reading the numbers

What each metric means, and which comparisons are fair to quote.

Service level and stockout

Stockout rate is the share of demand that could not be met from stock on hand; service level is its complement.

They are read alongside average inventory, because stockouts can always be driven to zero by holding unlimited stock.

Wastage and its control

Wastage reduction is measured against the agent stack without the Expiry Agent, not against the no-agent baseline.

The no-agent baseline wastes little only because it holds less stock and runs out more often, so comparing against it would measure service level, not redistribution.

Forecast error

MAPE is undefined at zero demand, so sMAPE and MASE carry the conclusions. MASE is scaled so seasonal-naive scores exactly 1.00.

On the shared protocol a moving average and the ensemble edge out the LSTM; the defensible claim is that the LSTM beats the seasonal baseline.

Detection

Anomalies are about 5% of shipments, so a detector that flags nothing scores 95% accuracy. Precision, recall and F1 are the useful columns.

Counterfeits change nothing observable in a shipment; only the ledger's fingerprint check catches them.
